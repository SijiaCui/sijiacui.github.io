I am a Ph.D. candidate (expected June 2027) at the [Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences](http://www.ia.ac.cn/), in the Key Laboratory of Cognition and Decision Intelligence for Complex Systems. I work on making large models **reason and act better through post-training**, with a bias toward ideas that survive contact with production-scale training.

I am seeking full-time roles in LLM post-training, RL and agents starting in 2027.

Research Interests
======
- **LLM post-training**: RLHF / RLVR, reward modeling, policy optimization
- **Reinforcement learning**: GRPO-family algorithms, sample efficiency, multi-task reward design
- **LLM agents**: tool use / function calling, memory, planning, multi-agent games
- **Test-time reasoning**: process verification, verifier-guided sampling

Currently I focus on RL post-training at scale, including multi-expert on-policy distillation (MOPD) for large LLMs.

Experience
======
- **Alibaba Qwen, RLHF team** (Nov 2025 – ): post-training algorithm intern. Multi-expert OPD model merging (35B–397B), self-play for VLM hallucination detection, and contrastive RLVR (CLIPO).
- **Zhipu AI** (Nov 2024 – Jul 2025): algorithm intern. Real-user function-calling benchmark and agent post-training with VERL.
